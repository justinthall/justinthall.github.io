An 8th- grade unit and lesson plan done for class during my junior year. The unit is a 5 day plan that teaches how characterization intersects with plot and ends with a creative "Book Tok" project. The lesson plan would be done on day 2, with the goal of refreshing students on characterization and preparing to consider plot in the mix on the next day.
