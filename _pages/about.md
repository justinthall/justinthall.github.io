# My Teaching Cornerstones


<div class="row">

  <div class="col-md-4">
    <h4>Cornerstone #1: Collaboration</h4>
    <p>
      I want to foster a collaborative classroom where students feel like they have a great amount of agency over their education. By fostering collaboration between myself and them, as well as between themselves, students will hopefully be more in charge of their time in my classroom. It also practices some very important social skills, while also being both fun and meaningful.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="col-md-4">
    <h4>Cornerstone #2: Flexibility</h4>
    <p>
      Above all else, I aim to be flexible. So much of this profession is based on responding to the needs and wants of others. I chafe when things are too rigid and too structured, and while these things are good for students, I think one can go just a bit too far. I also think flexibility is extremely important on a purely curriculum based level. A flexible classroom responds directly to what the students both want and need to learn, using data to be more effective.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="col-md-4">
    <h4>Cornerstone #3: Liberative</h4>
    <p>
      What's the point of education? To me, it's about becoming more able to understand the world around you and more willing to impact it for the better. Taking from Parker's Literacy is Liberation, our agency in this world is directly tied to our ability to read it. Not every student is going to use everything we teach in our ELA classes, but they will be able to hone their critical thinking and reading skills for whatever lies ahead.
    </p>
  </div>

</div>
